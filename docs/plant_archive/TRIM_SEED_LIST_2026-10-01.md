# Mike's trim seed list — 1 Oct 2026 (design §CC)

The designer's starting list for the archetype trim: per biome, four each of tree, shrub, grass, moss and fungi. **A seed, not a verdict:** the trim agents verify every binomial against Kew POWO, drop anything not drawable at 480p (yeasts, molds, rusts, smuts, slime molds, bacteria, algae, diatoms, lichens except as a fungus-category pick), re-sort ferns, cacti, kelp and epiphytes into the nine categories, and fill orchid, aroid, fern and cacti themselves. Where this list and the biome file disagree on the most archetypal species, prefer this list if the name is real and native to that kind of place.
