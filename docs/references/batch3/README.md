# Reference batch 3 — the §AG look (designer, 28 Sept 2026)

Five stills the designer attached (third-party reference clips from social media; for
internal art direction only, never shipped or redistributed): a sunset valley with a
robed figure on a bluff, a stone cottage on a hill path, a stone gate in a meadow with a
ring of paving, a graveyard pond, a lizard cook at a campfire. Cropped to the frame.

What they share (design §AG): tiny nearest-filtered tiles carrying all the detail, 5-bit
banded colour, blue pushed to ultramarine, navy shadows, thick sky-coloured fog, no cast
shadows, low camera. Measured statistics are in `data/look.json retro.targets` and the
measured colours in `retro.colors` (`tools/look/measure_look.py`).

`retro_tiles_preview.png` shows the generated tiles (`tools/look/make_retro_tiles.py`).
